The short version

The game has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email, phone number, or location. Your progress lives on your device. The only things that reach our servers are the leaderboard entries you choose to submit — a randomly generated ID, a display name you pick, and your time and mistake count.

The game is free and funded by ads from Google AdMob. You can opt out of personalised ads in the app's settings at any time.
