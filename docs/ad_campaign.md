# Google App Campaign: Sudoku (test run)

## Plan
- Countries: India, Philippines, Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt. Language: English.
- Budget: $5/day. End date: start + 5 days. Then stop and decide.
- Bidding: Maximize installs (no target CPI yet).
- Hard cap: manual payments (prepay ~$25-30), so Google can't charge more than that.
  - The daily budget can be overspent up to 2x on one day, but never more than
    30.4 x daily per month; the end date and the prepaid balance stop it for good.
